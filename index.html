About Me

I'm a Human-Centered Design graduate student at Pace University's Seidenberg School of Computer Science and Information Systems (MS, expected May 2027), with a background in Visual Communication Design from Ramapo College of New Jersey.

My work spans UX research, design thinking, and evidence-based product development. I'm especially interested in AI Product Management, Explainable AI, and Human-AI Collaboration — translating user insights into product requirements, facilitating cross-functional collaboration, and evaluating AI systems for usability, trust, and responsible deployment.
