About

I'm a graduate student in Human-Centered Design at Pace University's Seidenberg School. My work sits where research meets product: interviews, surveys, usability testing, and competitive analysis that end in a clear recommendation about what to build or change.

Before grad school I spent ten years as a visual merchandiser at Target, designing sales-floor experiences around how customers actually move and shop. I also hold a BA in Visual Communication Design with a minor in Creative Writing from Ramapo College. That mix of retail floor, visual design, and writing shapes how I run studies and how I report them.

Right now I'm most interested in AI product work: explainable AI, human-AI collaboration, and how the way an AI system communicates changes what people do with it.

Away from research: documentaries, history, tarot cards, knitting, and reading.
