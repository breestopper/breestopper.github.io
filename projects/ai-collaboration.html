AI as assistant, AI as collaborator Ongoing

Independent research that began as a comparison of Alexa, ChatGPT, and Gemini and now asks how working with AI changes the way people think.

Role
Independent researcher
Timeline
July 2026 – present
Methods
Comparative evaluation, prompt testing
Assistants
Amazon Alexa, ChatGPT, Google Gemini
Status
Ongoing

Where it started

This began as a side-by-side comparison of three popular AI assistants, Amazon Alexa, ChatGPT, and Google Gemini. I gave each the same questions, prompts, and conversational scenarios to see where they were strong, where they fell short, and which fit which kind of task.

Confirmation bias

One thread ran through all three: the way a question is worded shapes the answer. I looked for confirmation bias in the responses, examining how output shifted with the topic, the phrasing, and the surrounding conversation, and how easily an assistant would echo a belief back to the person who held it.

Where it is going

Comparing assistants raised a larger question that the project now follows: what changes when AI moves from an assistant that answers to a collaborator that thinks alongside you? I am interested in how working with AI shapes the way people reason, what they come to rely on it for, and where that helps or quietly narrows their thinking.

This is ongoing independent research. I will add structured findings as the work develops.
