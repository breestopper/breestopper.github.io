Mentage usability project

A 15-week collaboration with Mentage, a cognitive health app, covering competitive analysis and a research plan for its first year.

Role
Usability researcher
Partner
Mentage
Timeline
January – May 2026
Context
Usability project at Pace University

The brief

Mentage is building an app around memory and cognitive decline. Ahead of its release, our class worked with the team for a semester to evaluate the product and recommend where to take it.

Competitive analysis

I compared Mentage with similar apps to find where it stands apart and where it needed refinement before launch. The findings went to the Mentage team to inform product decisions.

A research plan for the first year

I also proposed a staged research roadmap so that each study builds on the one before it.

  1. Months 1 – 3: feasibility studyUse the clock drawing test and a short questionnaire about how patients feel about their memory, plus a questionnaire for a caregiver or loved one who sees the patient every day.
  2. Months 3 – 6: validation studyCheck that features do what they are intended to do, and observe how patients interact with the interface.
  3. Months 6 – 9: usability studyApply classification and regression tree analysis to see which activities target which cognitive abilities.
  4. Months 9 – 12: synthesisMap findings from all three studies side by side to find problems that repeat, then prioritize fixes.
  5. After 12 monthsRepeat the three studies whenever a new feature is considered.

Outcome

I completed the project with a certificate from the collaboration. The work taught me how to plan research for people who may be anxious about the thing being measured, where the wording of every prompt matters.
